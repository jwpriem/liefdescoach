## Review notes (English, for App Review Information)

    Yoga Ravennah is a yoga studio in Rotterdam, the Netherlands. This app lets the studio's members book and cancel lessons. The app is in Dutch.

    Sign in with the demo account supplied in the sign-in fields above. It has lesson credits.

    Payments: lessons are physical classes paid for in person at the studio. The studio then adds credits to the member's account. Credits cannot be bought in the app and the app contains no purchases (guideline 3.1.3(e), goods and services consumed outside the app).

    Booking: open "Boekingen", tap "Boek een les" and choose a lesson. A booking reserves a real place in a real class. A booking can be cancelled in the app up to 24 hours before the lesson; we remove any remaining test booking ourselves afterwards.

    Account deletion: open "Instellingen" and tap "Account verwijderen" at the bottom of the details card. Typing VERWIJDER confirms. Please do not delete the demo account itself if you want to keep testing with it; creating a new account in the app ("Registreren" on the login screen) is free.

    Notifications are optional and are switched on under "Instellingen" → "Pushberichten". They are lesson reminders and booking updates.

    Health details (injuries, pregnancy) are optional, entered by the member, and visible only to the studio.
